# {% include icon.html icon="fa-regular fa-envelope" %}Contact

Our lab is part of the Department of Molecular Medicine at the University of South Florida Morsani College of Medicine in beautiful Tampa, Florida. Feel free to contact us for job opportunities, potential collaborations, reagents, and more! 

## Prospective PhD students

The primary way join our lab as a PhD student is by being admitted to our umbrella [Medical Sciences PhD program](https://health.usf.edu/medicine/graduatestudies/phd), and then being accepted into our lab following a rotation after the fall of your first year. If you are in another PhD program on USF's campus and are interested in joining the lab, reach out to Krishna to determine options.

## Postdocs

We are currently accepting applications for postdoctoral positions! Please email Krishna with a CV and a statement of interest.

## Volunteers/Internships

We love hosting volunteers, but typically have limited spots. Still, feel free to email Krishna - even if spots in the lab are not available, he is always happy to chat and provide advice about how to get into research.
